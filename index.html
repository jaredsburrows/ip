🌍 Live globe

Opens a 3D globe of everyone here right now, and puts your own approximate area on it. The point is never finer than ~111 km, and is merged into a far larger region unless at least 5 people are sharing near you. “Stop sharing my area” inside the globe takes it off, and it disappears within 5 minutes of you leaving anyway. Nothing globe-related is downloaded until you press the button.
